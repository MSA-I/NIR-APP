WHY BANK RECONCILIATION IS NOT ONE OF THEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The data exists and the control centre counts it. What is missing is a
  DESTINATION this screen's readers can reach.

      routePolicy.ts:44   alerts: roles = ['owner', 'office']
      routePolicy.ts:42   bank:   roles = ['owner', 'accountant']

  A bank row on /alerts is a dead end for the office — the same defect DASH-04
  named in another form. The two repairs available were: widen /bank to the
  office, or widen nothing and say so. Widening a role list so that two surfaces
  agree is a privilege leak wearing the costume of a fix, so the screen names the
  omission in words instead (alerts.text_3, both dictionaries) and the count stays
  where its reader can act on it. `alertsControlCentreParity.spec.ts` asserts this
  as a rule rather than as a habit: every alert must land on a route every reader
  of /alerts may open, and it asserts that /bank genuinely fails that test.

TWO THINGS THE MEASUREMENT FOUND THAT THE FINDING DID NOT CLAIM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. THE TWO SURFACES COUNT PAYMENT REQUESTS OVER DIFFERENT POPULATIONS, and did
     so before this change.
         p2_payment_due_counts  — org-wide; status in (draft, pending_approval,
                                  approved, sent_for_execution).
         the snapshot           — narrowed by `p.unit_id is null or p.unit_id =
                                  any(auth_scopes())`; status NOT in (draft,
                                  executed, matched, cancelled).
     All 21 payment requests in this tenant carry a unit_id, so the narrowing is
     live rather than theoretical; the owner's four scopes happen to cover them,
     which is why both readings answer 2 today. NOTHING WAS WIDENED to reconcile
     them. The new rows are read from the snapshot — the NARROWER of the two — and
     the two whose figure is unit-scoped say so in their own detail line
     ("נספרות היחידות שבתחום האחריות שלך בלבד"). Recorded, not silently repaired.

  2. THE PRICE-INCREASE ROWS ALSO COUNT DIFFERENT THINGS: the control centre counts
     SUPPLIERS who raised a price, inside the currency the picker is on; the alert
     counts OFFERS, across every currency. Both are 1 here. Left alone for the same
     reason — each is honest about its own population, and this finding is about
     conditions that appear on one surface and not the other, not about two
     honest labels.
