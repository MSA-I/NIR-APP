RESIDUAL, RECORDED RATHER THAN SILENTLY FIXED
----------------------------------------------
The new hand-off assertion covers the `fileName={...}` spelling. A file name passed into a
component under some OTHER prop name is a transfer it does not see; widening it to every
`attr={...}` would sweep in `src=`, `alt=` and `message=` and drown the signal. The guard states
this in its own comment, with the reason, so the next reader does not have to rediscover it.
