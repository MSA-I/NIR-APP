3. THE MECHANISM — three policies in deliberate agreement, not one bug
================================================================================

`credit_request_balance_rows` is SECURITY INVOKER (prosecdef = false) and admits
`auth_role() in ('owner','accountant')`, so the function itself is not the gate. The gate is RLS:

  credit_requests_select            org_id = auth_org() AND role IN (owner, office, accountant)
                                    -- no approval predicate here at all

  credit_requests_derived_scope_rider (cmd ALL)
      (invoice_id IS NOT NULL OR receipt_item_id IS NOT NULL)
      AND (invoice_id IS NULL OR EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM invoices i WHERE ... i.unit_id = ANY(auth_scopes())))
      AND (receipt_item_id IS NULL OR EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM goods_receipt_items gri JOIN goods_receipts gr ... ))

THE POINT: those EXISTS subqueries read `invoices` and `goods_receipts` under the CALLER's own
RLS. They are not exempt.

  invoices_select        ... OR (auth_role() = 'accountant' AND review_status = 'approved')
  goods_receipts_select  ... OR (auth_role() = 'accountant' AND EXISTS (invoice_receipt_links
                                 -> invoices i WHERE i.review_status = 'approved'))

So the accountant's horizon is "approved invoices and what hangs off them", enforced identically
in three places. A credit anchored to an unapproved invoice fails the first EXISTS; a credit
anchored to a receipt with no approved invoice fails the second. Both legs close.

THIS IS DECIDED PRODUCT BEHAVIOUR, NOT A DEFECT. docs/OPEN-DECISIONS.md #13:
  "תפקיד accountant — הנהלת חשבונות תפעולית: חשבוניות מאושרות והקשר רכש מינימלי בלבד"
  (operational bookkeeping: APPROVED INVOICES and minimal purchasing context only).

5. WHY NEITHER OF THE TWO OBVIOUS FIXES WAS TAKEN
================================================================================

(a) SCOPE THE QUOTED FIGURE TO THE READER. Refused. `open_credit_override_total` is an immutable
    audit fact with a table constraint binding it to a reason and a timestamp
    (payment_requests_open_credit_override_complete, 0073). Recomputing it per reader would
    destroy the record of what the approver saw and, for this role, always yield 0 — deleting the
    very warning that exists to tell the executor an exception was taken. That removes a control.

(b) WIDEN THE READER SO THE LIST MATCHES. Refused. That is the privilege leak the plan forbids,
    and ruling #13 already decided the opposite. It would disclose unapproved-invoice activity to
    the accountant, and it would have to be done in three policies at once.

The two figures differ because their POPULATIONS differ — the approver's view then, this
reader's view now — so the plan's own rule applies: the label states its scope. That is a client
and dictionary change. Nothing in the database is wrong.

RESIDUAL, recorded rather than silently decided: whether the accountant should be shown the
override AMOUNT at all, as opposed to only the fact of the override and its reason, is a product
question this PR did not settle. It kept the amount, because removing a money figure from an
approval-override audit note is a larger decision than a display fix. If the owner would rather
the figure did not appear on a screen that cannot corroborate it, that is a one-line follow-up
in the same Note.
