the pre-spend check, section 6 for the locked write), so the override is unreachable at both.

WHY THIS ROW IS BLOCKED RATHER THAN FIXED: the fix is a change to a SECURITY DEFINER function

WHAT THE FIX HAS TO DECIDE, and it is a business question rather than a mechanical one:
whether an override BEATS the introductory allowance or is merely compared with it. The
introductory allowance is 50 runs over 30 days; a plan override could be lower. Taking the
greater of the two is the only reading under which "grant this customer more" always means
more — but it is an owner ruling, not a migration's choice, and OPEN-DECISIONS is where it
belongs before the body changes.
