WHAT I READ IN THE IMAGES, which is the part a probe cannot assert

  Over-allocated. The grey summary panel at the top of the dialog carries two rows in one column:
  the line's own 2,950.00 in bold on the first, and 5,900.00 beneath it against the label
  "משויך מהתנועה (מאושר)". Directly under the panel, a rose band states the excess. It sits ABOVE
  the existing amber note about what un-matching does, so the money discrepancy is read before the
  procedural explanation rather than after it. The whole dialog fits without scrolling and the
  primary "הסרת התאמה" is still reachable — the alert informs, it does not block, which is the
  right shape for a screen whose job is recording what the bank already did.

  Split that adds up. The same panel, the same two rows — 2,950.00 over 2,950.00 — and NO rose
  band anywhere. The amber note moves up into the space the alert would have taken. A screen that
  flagged an ordinary two-invoice split would be crying wolf, and it does not.

  Both are RTL throughout: labels start at the right edge, the figures are in the `num` column at
  the left, and the Hebrew reads correctly with no bidi damage around the ₪ or the digits.
