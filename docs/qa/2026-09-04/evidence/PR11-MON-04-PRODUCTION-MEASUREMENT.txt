THE FINDING SAYS: "One 2,950.00 ILS statement line carries two CONFIRMED allocations of 2,950.00
each to the same invoice." THAT IS NOT WHAT IS THERE, and the difference decides the design.

--- 1. how many lines are over-allocated, across all tenants -----------------------------------

  with per_tx as (
    select t.org_id, t.id, t.currency, round(t.amount, 3) as tx_amount,
           round(coalesce(sum(ba.amount) filter (where ba.confirmed), 0), 3) as confirmed_sum,
           count(*) filter (where ba.confirmed) as confirmed_rows,
           round(coalesce(sum(ba.amount), 0), 3) as any_sum,
           count(ba.id) as any_rows

WHAT THAT MEANS. The first row is the demo seed's SUGGESTION — it is written verbatim at
`supabase/demo/demo_seed.sql:758`, `confirmed false`, on a line the same seed leaves `suggested`.
The second is a real `match_bank_transaction` call made on 02.09.2026 (the sweep itself), which
inserted a confirmed allocation and set the line to `matched`. ONE claim on the money, not two.

TWO CONSEQUENCES, BOTH DELIBERATE:

  1. The bound in `0322` counts CONFIRMED rows and only those. A suggestion claims no money, and
     the product already reads the table that way everywhere (`Bank.tsx:502`,
     `match_bank_transaction`'s idempotent replay, `unmatch_bank_transaction`). A bound that
     counted suggestions would refuse the ordinary path: confirming a full-line suggestion inserts
     a SECOND row rather than flipping the first, so this very line could never have been matched.

  2. `0322`'s pre-flight assertion — that no line is already over the bound — is safe to apply to
     production: the confirmed sum here is EQUAL to the line, and equal is allowed.

RESIDUAL, NOT FIXED HERE. `match_bank_transaction` does not clear the suggestions on a line it
confirms, so every confirmed match leaves its suggestion behind and the line then displays two
rows of the same amount. That is the appearance the finding described. It is a data-hygiene defect
in a financial command, its correction would be a deletion of financial rows, and which row
survives is the owner's call. The pair is left exactly as it is.
