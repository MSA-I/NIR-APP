   `expect(imported.length).toBeGreaterThanOrEqual(6)` and, worse,
   `expect(rowsWhere(cell => cell.includes(IMPORT_REASON)).length)

   row, never by count"). The per-row loop beside them —
   `for (const cells of imported) expect(...).not.toContain('לא נרשמה סיבה')` —
   is what saves the test. The second `>= 6` would be satisfied by six rows
   carrying the reason while a seventh silently did not.

   dictionary asserting itself: it pins a literal to the entry that literal was
   copied from and cannot fail for any product reason. It is harmless here
   because the two `getByText` render assertions beside it do the real work — but
   it is the kind of line that reads as evidence later and is not.
