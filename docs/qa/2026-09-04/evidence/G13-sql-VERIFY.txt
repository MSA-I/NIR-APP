4. THE RACE IN p115 IS PROVED, NOT ASSERTED IN SHAPE
-----------------------------------------------------
p115 records the two session outcomes into p115.results and drops its own schema on success, so
after a green run the two strings cannot be read. Two independent readings were taken instead.

(a) FROM THE COMMITTED DATA of the green run, which the drop does not touch, because the
    fixtures live in public tables:
      f115...0047  race that overflows, 2,000 + 2,000 against 2,950.00 -> 1 row, 2000.000
      f115...0048  race that fits,      1,000 + 1,000 against 2,950.00 -> 2 rows, 2000.000
    Exactly one winner where the two overflow, both standing where they fit. So the guard's lock
    is not simply refusing whoever arrives second.

(b) A SUPPLEMENTARY PROBE — not a change to p115 — repeating the identical race on a NEW
    statement line built from p115's own leftover fixtures, and PRINTING both outcomes:
        step      | outcome
        ----------+----------------------------------------
        session_a | ok
        session_b | bank_allocation_exceeds_statement_line
        allocation_rows | total
        --------------- + ---------
                      1 | 2000.000
    Session B was refused BY NAME. Not a deadlock, not a statement timeout — the probe sets
    statement_timeout to 20 s and session A holds the row lock through a 1.2 s sleep inside its
    transaction. The probe's fixture was removed by the reset that followed.
