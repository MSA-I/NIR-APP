Exit 0 is the whole assertion, and this is worth saying out loud: `0320` raises rather than warns.
Its two anchored replacements each assert their occurrence count is exactly 1, and its
`$assert_0320$` block re-reads `pg_proc` afterwards. A reset that reaches "Finished" is a reset in
which

  * `left join balances b on b.invoice_id = i.id`                      occurred exactly once,
  * `coalesce(sum(b.balance_in_currency), 0)::numeric(14,3),`          occurred exactly once,
  * the positive sentinel `sum(b.balance_in_currency)::numeric(14,3)`  is present afterwards,
  * `left join balances` and `coalesce(sum(b.balance_in_currency)`     are both gone,
  * the function is still `SECURITY DEFINER` with `search_path=public` pinned, and still carries
    EXECUTE for `authenticated` and `service_role`,
  * `private.scope_enforcement_violations()` returned nothing.

Read back independently of the migration's own opinion:

    no-left-join | sentinel-present | no-coalesce | secdef=true
    body_md5   = 24a2e15a89bf816f3cf0a290860e3771     (LF-normalised)
    proconfig  = search_path=public
    A5 row `p0_supplier_balance_rows_by_currency()` — body_hash matches pg_proc: true
    private.scope_enforcement_violations() -> 0 rows

The A5 pin is recomputed from `pg_proc` by the migration, never typed as a literal, so it cannot
carry a CRLF-flavoured digest across machines.

Every one of those readings was taken with `role` set to `authenticated` and a real
`request.jwt.claim.sub`, through the VIEW the client reads (`public.supplier_balances_by_currency`),
so `auth_org()`, `auth_role()`, `auth_scopes()`, RLS and the view's grants were all in the path. A
reading taken as `postgres` would have skipped all of them and proved nothing.

FIN-04'S OWN DIAGNOSIS IS INVERTED, AND THE RED IS WHERE THAT IS VISIBLE. It recorded "a phantom
row on a supplier with no dollar activity". In the same run, the owner reads `owner_a_usd =
300.000/1` — the dollar activity exists and the supplier owes the money. What the accountant's
screen printed was not a phantom row; it was a FALSE STATEMENT OF SETTLEMENT over a real debt,
shown to the one person whose job is to reconcile against a bank statement. `MON-03` is right.
