## What was measured and found correct — the regression baseline

**This is the list the next sweep measures against.** It is here because it is the half of a sweep
that vanishes: a rollout takes these away in silence, nobody looks for them, and the loss is found
by a customer. A finding is a thing to fix; this is a thing to keep.

**Money.** Over-payment refused by the server. A partial payment of 300 ILS left exactly 340, and
the completing payment landed on **an exact zero**. A duplicate payment was refused. Currency
separation was not violated on any screen, in any file, or in any assistant answer. A price
snapshot held, including after a supplier's counter-offer. Bank matching and its reversal both pass
with a reason recorded word for word.

**Permissions.** Office receives **zero rows** from payments, from the bank and from allocations —
a real server refusal, not a hidden screen. Every write attempted by an unauthorised role was
refused. The operator console is **sealed to all three tenant roles**. A request for an id outside
the organisation returns an empty list, not an error that admits the row exists.

**Files.** The accountant's monthly report reconciles to the agora across three months and two
roles. The expenses export prints 27,467 ILS and 300 USD as two separate figures, in the PDF too.
The price-snapshot label survives into the order's PDF. **All 14 downloaded files were scanned for
secrets — clean.**

**Settings.** Organisation name, day range and two tolerance settings: all saved, all survived a
reload, **and all were restored**.

**Entrance.** The error message is identical for a registered and an unregistered address. All 14
screens that require a link from an e-mail refuse gracefully both with no link and with a forged
one. The site is still blocked from search engines.

Every remediation PR in `docs/GATES.md` is measured against this list as well as against its own
oracle. A fix that closes a finding and breaks a line above it has not closed anything.
